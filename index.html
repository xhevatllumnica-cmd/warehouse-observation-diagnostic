Xhevat Llumnica · Reports directly to Shqipron Sezallari · August 2026

The job in one sentence

You run the warehouse floor: goods coming in, orders picked, packed, and shipped out, and the team that makes that happen every day.

Your 3 targets

Rule zero, above all three: nobody gets hurt. Safety beats speed, every time.

1Every order placed today ships today

On a normal day customers place around 1,000 orders. Your target: all of them completed the same day. On a smaller day, every single one. Nothing carries over to tomorrow.

2Lower the cost per order

Cost per order = total warehouse cost divided by orders shipped. In your first month you measure it. Then we agree a target together and you bring it down step by step: smarter shift planning, less packaging waste, fewer mistakes that need redoing.

3Run a disciplined team

Shifts start on time, fully staffed. Every picking or packing mistake is tracked to a person and fixed the same week. Not tolerated: showing up late or not at all without notice, safety violations, touching inventory outside the rules.
